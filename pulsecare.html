02 — Problem & Background

Poorly designed medical software and fragmented ecosystems lead to critical human errors, rising costs, and inaccessible healthcare delivery.

Error Risk

System inefficiencies, complex EHR interfaces, and unclear medication dosage displays frequently cause clinical errors and patient harm.

The Solution Approach

PulseCare simplifies complex clinical workflows, minimizes cognitive load for practitioners, and provides transparent, accessible booking and health tracking for patients.

System Inefficiencies

Slow systems with constant login requirements and manual data entry cause user frustration and mistakes.

Mental Model Mismatches

Interfaces that fail to align with how doctors naturally work cause incorrect data input and workflow disruptions.

Complex Device Interfaces

Cluttered touchscreens with poorly designed buttons and ambiguous alerts threaten patient safety.

Confusing dosage displays and ambiguous labeling in Electronic Health Records are directly implicated in preventable medication overdoses and medical errors.

11 — Reflection

Key Learnings & Impact

Designing PulseCare highlighted the extreme importance of error prevention in healthcare UX. When software impacts clinical decisions and prescription handling, clarity and fail-safes are non-negotiable.

Clarity Over Clutter

Eliminating ambiguous labels and streamlining EHR data entry directly reduces clinical fatigue and prescription errors.

Patient-Centric Access

Bridging patients with verified specialists through seamless booking creates trust and improves overall healthcare delivery.
