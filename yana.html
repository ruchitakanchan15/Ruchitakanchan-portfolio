02 — Problem & Background

Mental wellness apps can feel overwhelming and impersonal, making it difficult for young adults to express emotions and seek support.

60%+

of young adults experience stress related to academics, relationships or everyday pressures.

The outcome

YANA uses a calm, supportive experience to make emotional wellbeing easier to navigate. Mood check-ins, journaling and guided activities help users reflect, regulate emotions and access support without feeling overwhelmed.

Emotional Expression

Users may find it difficult to put their feelings into words during stressful moments.

Complex Experiences

Too many features and complicated interfaces can make wellness apps feel overwhelming.

Lack of Personalization

Generic suggestions may not match the user's emotional state or individual needs.

Users don't always need advice. Sometimes they need a simple, private space where they can understand and express what they're feeling.

— YANA User Research

11 — Reflection

What I learned

Working on YANA changed how I approach UI/UX design. Good UX doesn't just solve a problem — it makes the user feel understood while solving it.

Empathy First

Designing for emotional vulnerability requires stripping away unnecessary cognitive load and visual noise.

Simplicity Matters

When users are stressed, micro-interactions and calm color palettes keep them engaged without feeling overwhelmed.
